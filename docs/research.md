## Scope and claims

The owned version handles internal casework records and human review evidence. No statutory eligibility engine, applicant portal, financial award, payment execution or automated notice is included. A customer needing those functions needs a scoped and tested implementation with the responsible authority. Ten example questions show current CLI capability, not a substantiated assertion that Salesforce cannot answer them. Three page examples are captured directly from fictional seeded data.
